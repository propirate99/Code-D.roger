🏥 Offline Travel First-Aid Protocols

☀️ Heatstroke & Dehydration

Move to shaded area immediately, sip cold water with ORS, and apply wet towels to neck and forehead.

🏔️ Altitude Sickness (AMS)

Descend immediately by 500-1000m, drink warm fluids, avoid alcohol, and breathe deeply.

🩹 Severe Bleeding & Cuts

Apply firm continuous pressure with clean cloth, elevate injured limb above heart, and do not remove embedded objects.
